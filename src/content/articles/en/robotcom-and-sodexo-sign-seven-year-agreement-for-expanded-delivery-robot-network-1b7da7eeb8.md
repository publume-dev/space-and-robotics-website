- Robot.com announced a seven-year commercial agreement with Sodexo Group, building on a partnership that began in 2021 across Sodexo-operated college campuses in North America.
- The seven-year contract is Robot.com's largest single enterprise deployment.
- Robot.com's R-Kiwi robot has completed 2.4 million tasks across campuses and city sidewalks.
- More than 500 R-Kiwi delivery robots have been deployed.
- In the first half of 2026, robots generated revenue for a quarter of a million hours, with a goal to reach a million hours per year soon.
- Robot.com plans to add the R-Dog quadruped robot to its delivery fleet, which can drop packages and go upstairs or enter autonomous vehicles without modification.
