- CesiumAstro is acquiring semiconductor specialist Jariet Technologies for an undisclosed sum, bringing in about 100 employees across operations in the United States and Switzerland.
- Jariet Technologies designs radio-frequency chips used in satellite communications, electronic warfare and radar systems, including technology for digital beamforming and wideband signal processing.
- The acquisition is intended to support CesiumAstro's future satellite constellation and is a big part of enabling that constellation.
- CesiumAstro filed plans with regulators last month for its Synchronicity low Earth orbit network, covering 737 satellites that would use Ku-, Ka- and V-band spectrum.
- The first Element satellite platform is slated to launch in October 2026, with more scheduled on seven additional SpaceX rideshare missions booked over the following several years.
