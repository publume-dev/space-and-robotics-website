- The White House released an updated National Space Transportation Policy on Aug 20, 2026, setting a goal to support at least 1,000 launches and reentries per year from U.S. facilities by 2030.
- The policy directs the Department of Defense, NASA, and the Department of Transportation to identify potential locations for additional launch facilities and targeted development or improvement of launch infrastructure.
- The policy directs the Pentagon and NASA to develop scheduling criteria for federal launch facilities within 180 days to maximize use for commercial and federal operations.
- The policy directs the Department of the Interior, working with other agencies, to identify federal lands within 90 days that could be used as additional federal reentry sites.
- The policy directs the Office of Science and Technology Policy to coordinate a space transportation industrial base strategy within 180 days, with an emphasis on workforce development.
- The policy calls for a review of export policies and programs to support the U.S. launch industry within 120 days and every two years thereafter.
- The policy directs NASA to expand commercial space transportation beyond Earth orbit, including a lunar logistics architecture and commercial architectures for sending humans to the surface of Mars and returning them to Earth.
