- Locus Robotics acquired Nexera Robotics, known for its NeuraGrasp gripping technology, and plans to integrate the soft gripper into its Array mobile manipulator, which was unveiled in March 2025 with a suction-cup gripper.
- Roy Belak, SVP of Robotic Grasping at Locus, expects the next public display of Array to feature Nexera technology, though no exact timeline was given.
- Nexera's NeuraGrasp technology combines suction and pinch grasping to handle a wider range of items, including porous polybags and cloth items.
