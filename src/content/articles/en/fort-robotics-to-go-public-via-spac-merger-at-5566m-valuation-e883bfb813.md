- FORT Robotics announced a merger with SPAC Newbury Street II Acquisition Corp. to form FORT Robotics Holdings Inc., listing on Nasdaq at a valuation of $556.6 million.
- The transaction is expected to deliver approximately $201 million in gross proceeds, including $31 million from PIPE and non-redemption agreement investments from Tiger Global, Prologis Ventures, and Mark Cuban.
- FORT Robotics reported 2025 revenue growth of 62% year-over-year, with 91% growth among customers spending over $100,000 annually, and maintained standalone gross margins of 66% in 2025.
- The merger is expected to close in Q4 2026, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals.
