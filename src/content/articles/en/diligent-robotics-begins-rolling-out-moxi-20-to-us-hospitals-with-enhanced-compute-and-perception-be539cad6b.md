- Diligent Robotics has begun rolling out Moxi 2.0 to health system customers across the US, including Endeavor Health Edward Hospital, Providence Saint John’s Health Center, and Children’s Hospital Los Angeles.
- Moxi 2.0 features 10x onboard compute and 10-15x faster perception compared to the previous generation, along with a new Robotic World Model.
- Moxi 2.0 can operate up to 18 hours per day with 9 hours of continuous runtime and 30% faster charging.
- Children’s Hospital Los Angeles reported that Moxi completed more than 40,000 deliveries, representing over 16,000 hours of staff work saved, and its fleet expanded from two to three robots.
- Moxi 2.0 was developed using NVIDIA Isaac Sim and NVIDIA Cosmos, and was trained on AWS SageMaker HyperPod with network support from T-Mobile for Business.
- Diligent designed Moxi 2.0 with modularity to continue using the Fetch base or switch to another base in the future.
