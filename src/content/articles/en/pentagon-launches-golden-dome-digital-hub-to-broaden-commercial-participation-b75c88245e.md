- The Pentagon created the Golden Dome for America Ecosystem Hub, a digital portal for companies, startups, investors, and universities to submit concepts and access unclassified information.
- Gen. Michael Guetlein, leading the program, has met with more than 500 companies; the hub provides an unclassified starting point for submissions before formal solicitations.
- A screening process and security group have been established to help protect intellectual property and IT systems.
- A shared data environment for government, approved contractors, and academia includes data and AI sandboxes for analytics.
- Twelve teams are developing prototypes for the space-based interceptor program, using an acquisition model requiring early self-funding by contractors.
- Two companies are under contract for supply-chain risk analysis: one examining deep-tier bottlenecks, the other identifying alternative supply sources.
