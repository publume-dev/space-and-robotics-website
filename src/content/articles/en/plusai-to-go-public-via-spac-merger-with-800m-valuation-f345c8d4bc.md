- PlusAI is set to go public through a merger with SPAC Texas Ventures Acquisition III Corp., valuing the company at $800 million pre-money.
- The deal is supported by up to $300 million in capital, including more than $60 million from Yorkville Advisors and about $236 million from Texas Ventures III trust funding.
- PlusAI generates $25 million in revenue via its HyperFoundry platform and targets $40-50 million in contracted revenue in 2026.
- PlusAI is operating autonomous freight routes with Ryder and International Motors in Texas.
- PlusAI plans to use the merger proceeds for commercialization, targeting a 2027 launch of factory-built autonomous trucks with SuperDrive.
- The merger is expected to close in 2026, subject to customary conditions, and has been approved by both boards.

If completed, the merger is expected to provide PlusAI with additional capital to advance its autonomous trucking commercialization, including the planned 2027 launch of factory-built trucks.

Watch for progress on the expected 2026 deal closing and whether the 2027 production timeline is met.
