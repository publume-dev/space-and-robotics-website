- ESA signed the first contracts for its European Launcher Challenge, awarding Rocket Factory Augsburg €186.9 million, PLD Space €158.9 million, and Isar Aerospace €197.8 million to develop new launch services.
- The European Launcher Challenge requires selected launch service providers to achieve an orbital launch before 2028 to demonstrate capability.
- Rocket Factory Augsburg plans to launch its RFA One vehicle from SaxaVord in the UK, targeting up to 500 kg to a 500 km sun-synchronous orbit, with the contract mainly funded by Germany and a contribution from the UK.
- PLD Space will launch its Miura 5 from Europe's Spaceport in French Guiana, designed to deliver up to 540 kg to sun-synchronous orbit, with the contract mainly funded by Spain and a contribution from Germany.
- Isar Aerospace's Spectrum launcher, which first flew in 2025 for 30 seconds, targets up to 1000 kg to low Earth orbit, and its second vehicle is being prepared for a qualification flight with payloads from Andøya Spaceport.

The contracts aim to strengthen Europe's independent access to space, supporting commercial launch service providers.

The contract award process with MaiaSpace is nearing completion and is expected to resume in the coming weeks, with ESA providing an update once concluded.
