- NASA has modernized its CEA code into CEA v3, a Fortran 2008 object-oriented software package with expanded interface support and updated thermochemical data.
- CEA v3 supports interfaces for Fortran, C, Python, MATLAB, and Excel, and includes backward compatibility through a legacy command-line interface.
- The thermodynamic database has been expanded to include green propellant constituents such as ADN, HAN, and LMP-103S, and sustainable aviation fuel candidates such as n-Butanol.
- In one benchmark, a sweep of 108,500 cases completed in approximately 1.11 seconds with CEA v3 compared to about 15 minutes with CEA2, an approximately 800-fold reduction in runtime.
