- President Trump signed an executive order to establish the U.S. Space Academy, the nation's first such institution, to educate and train space professionals.
- NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman will chair a new Presidential Commission on the U.S. Space Academy, with NASA Deputy Administrator Matt Anderson serving as executive director.
- The commission must deliver a report to the President within 120 days of the signing, with recommendations on governance, service obligations, location, and legislative requirements.
