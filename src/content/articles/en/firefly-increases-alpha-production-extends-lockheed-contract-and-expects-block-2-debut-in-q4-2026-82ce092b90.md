- **Alpha production at an all-time high.** Firefly Aerospace says its Alpha launch vehicle production is at an "all-time high" and is ramping up.
- **Block 2 debut moves to Q4 2026.** The first Block 2 Alpha launch, Flight 8, is now expected in the fourth quarter of 2026, after previously being scheduled for late summer.
- **2026 launch plan trimmed.** Firefly now plans only one more Alpha launch after Flight 8 in 2026, down from two launches projected in May.
- **Lockheed Martin contract extended.** Lockheed's Alpha launch contract now runs through 2031, with the maximum number of launches unchanged at 25.
- **Miranda engine test completed.** Firefly completed a mission-duty-cycle test of the Miranda engine for the Eclipse vehicle, firing it for 226 seconds.
- **True Anomaly launch deferred.** The Alpha launch for True Anomaly's Victus Haze mission is saved for a later date per Space Force direction, after the Jackal spacecraft launched on a SpaceX rideshare.
