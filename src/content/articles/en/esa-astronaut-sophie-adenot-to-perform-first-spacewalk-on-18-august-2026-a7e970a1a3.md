- ESA astronaut Sophie Adenot will perform her first spacewalk on 18 August 2026, together with NASA astronaut Anil Menon.
- The spacewalk will begin at approximately 13:35 BST / 14:35 CEST, exiting from the Quest airlock on the ISS.
- The primary task is to replace the Space-to-Ground Antenna (SGANT), a critical communication system for NASA.
- The EVA duration is expected to be about six and a half hours.
- Sophie Adenot is the fifth French astronaut to perform a spacewalk; this is the 12th spacewalk by a French astronaut and the 31st by a European astronaut.
