- iSpace has secured a first batch of series E funding of nearly $148 million (1 billion yuan), according to STCN.
- The funding will go towards research and mass production of the Hyperbola-3 reusable launch vehicle, Focus-2 engines, launch and sea recovery infrastructure, and pre-research on heavy-lift launch vehicles.
- iSpace plans the debut flight of Hyperbola-3 before the end of 2026, with an attempted first stage recovery as part of the mission.
- iSpace launched what it described as China's first rocket recovery ship in August 2025, named 'Interstellar Return', designed to recover the first stage of Hyperbola-3 and other reusable rockets.
- iSpace plans to list on the STAR Market in the second half of 2027, following the expected debut of Hyperbola-3.
