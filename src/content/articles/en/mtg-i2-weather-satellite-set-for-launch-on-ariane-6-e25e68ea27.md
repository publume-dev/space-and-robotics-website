- ESA's live coverage of the MTG-I2 launch on flight VA270 starts at 21:42 CEST on Thursday, 27 August, with launch expected at 22:10 CEST.
- MTG-I2 is the third satellite in the MTG constellation, completing the mission's first family of satellites.
- MTG-I2 carries a Lightning Imager and a Flexible Combined Imager, enabling scans of Europe and North Africa every 2.5 minutes.
- This will be the ninth launch of Ariane 6 and its first mission to geostationary transfer orbit, the farthest destination for the rocket so far.
