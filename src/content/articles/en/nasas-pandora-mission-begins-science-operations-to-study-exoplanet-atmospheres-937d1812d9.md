- Pandora, the first Astrophysics Pioneers program satellite, began science operations after launch on January 11 and successful commissioning.
- It will determine the atmospheric composition of at least 20 exoplanets, including hazes, clouds, and water.
- The spacecraft uses a novel all-aluminum telescope about 18 inches in diameter and observes targets simultaneously in visible and infrared light.
- During its year-long primary mission, Pandora will observe each of at least 20 exoplanets ten times, with each observation lasting 24 hours and including a transit.
