- BepiColombo's Mercury Transfer Module (MTM) will separate from the two science orbiters on 3 September 2026, marking the start of the Mercury Arrival Phase.
- After MTM separation, the Mercury Planetary Orbiter (MPO) and JAXA's Mercury Magnetospheric Orbiter (Mio) will enter orbit around Mercury together in November 2026 and separate from each other in December 2026.
- Science operations for both BepiColombo orbiters are scheduled to begin in April 2027.
- ESA will livestream the MTM separation operations from 13:45 CEST on 3 September 2026, with the separation scheduled for 14:00 CEST and earliest expected acquisition of signal at 15:53 CEST.
