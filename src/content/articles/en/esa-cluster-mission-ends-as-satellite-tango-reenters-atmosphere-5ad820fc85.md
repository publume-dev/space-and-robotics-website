- The ESA Cluster mission ended when Tango reentered Earth's atmosphere on 1 September 2026 at 23:30:31 CEST.
- The ROSIE airborne observation team observed both the Samba and Tango reentries with 29 of the 30 instruments onboard their plane.
- ESA's Draco reentry mission is scheduled for launch in 2027 to record what happens to a satellite during reentry from inside, using over two hundred sensors, four cameras, and an indestructible capsule.
