- Unitree Robotics debuted on Shanghai's STAR Market, closing its first day 460% higher and raising 6.1 billion yuan (~$905 million).
- Humanoid robot sales grew from 5 in 2023 to 5,215 in 2025, with around 70% going to universities and research institutions.
- The company reported a profit of about $41 million in 2025, up from $14 million in 2024 and a $1.6 million loss in 2023.
- In July 2026, the U.S. FCC restricted imports of certain foreign-produced robots, including mobile robots such as humanoids and quadrupeds, affecting Unitree's future sales in the U.S.
