- Rocket Lab warned on an Aug. 10 earnings call that a first Neutron launch before the end of 2026 is narrowing in probability, potentially slipping into 2027.
- The company announced a contract to launch Kepler Communications satellites on Neutron no earlier than 2028.
- CEO Peter Beck said Rocket Lab plans a one-three-five launch cadence for Neutron in its first three years, based on Electron experience.

- Watch for: Neutron flight hardware is moving into final checkout and assembly, targeting delivery to the pad in Q4 2026, followed by pad tests including a static-fire of the first stage before launch.
