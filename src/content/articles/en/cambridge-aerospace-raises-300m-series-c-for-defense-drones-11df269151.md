- Cambridge Aerospace raised $300 million in Series C funding, bringing its valuation to $3.4 billion.
- The company plans to expand manufacturing capacity and invest in spiral development of existing products.
- Cambridge Aerospace holds U.K. Ministry of Defence contracts, including one for low-cost interceptors for the British Armed Forces; specific contract values and terms were not disclosed.
- The first product, Skyhammer, has a range of over 30 km and a top speed of 700 km/h.
- Cambridge Aerospace projects that its next product, Starhammer, will reach the market in 2027; this timeline is a company projection and may change.
