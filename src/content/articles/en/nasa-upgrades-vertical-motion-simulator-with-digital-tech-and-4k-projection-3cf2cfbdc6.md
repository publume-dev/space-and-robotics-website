- NASA's Vertical Motion Simulator at Ames Research Center has been upgraded, replacing analog systems with modern digital technology and adding a dome with advanced 4K projectors for nearly 20/20 visual clarity.
- The new system can automatically align and color-match images into a simulated background, a process that previously took hours or days.
- The simulator can now use lighter, removable inserts instead of swapping entire cabs, cutting configuration time in half.

- The upgrades are expected to streamline mission preparation, though the article does not provide a timeline for completion or upcoming missions that will use the simulator.
