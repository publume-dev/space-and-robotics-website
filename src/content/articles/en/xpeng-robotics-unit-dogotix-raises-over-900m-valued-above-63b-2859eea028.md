- Funding: Dogotix raised over $900 million in its initial round, with a pre-money valuation of $5 billion and post-money valuation above $6.3 billion.
- Production plan: It plans to produce 1,000 IRON humanoids per month by end of 2026, with commercial deliveries starting in 2027.
- Investors: IDG Capital led the round; strategic investors include Alibaba and Tencent, with Gaorong Ventures participating.
- Industry record: XPeng says the funding sets a record for a single private financing in China's embodied AI sector.
- Structure: Dogotix remains a subsidiary of XPeng, with He Xiaopeng staying CEO of both.
