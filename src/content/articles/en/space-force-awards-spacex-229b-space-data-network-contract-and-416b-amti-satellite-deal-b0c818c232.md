- In May 2026, the Space Force awarded SpaceX a $2.29 billion contract to build the initial backbone of the Space Data Network; a fully operational prototype is expected by the end of 2027.
- SpaceX was separately selected for a $4.16 billion contract to build the initial constellation for the space-based Airborne Moving Target Indicator (AMTI) program.
- In late July 2026, the Space Force placed a $1.6 billion order for 18 Falcon 9 launches under National Security Space Launch Phase 3 Lane 1 to deploy satellites for the Space-Based Sensing and Targeting portfolio through the end of 2027.
- SpaceX says Starshield is a rapidly growing government business, with more than $6 billion in multiyear awards for military communications and sensing networks.
- The Space Force announced $615 million in contracts to Rocket Lab, Systems & Technology Research, and a third undisclosed company under a second AMTI task order.
