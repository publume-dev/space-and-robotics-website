- NVIDIA announced the Jetson Orin Nano 2, an entry-level edge AI computer featuring 78 TOPS of AI compute, 8GB memory, and an eight-core Arm CPU.
- It doubles the inference performance of the Jetson Orin Nano Super while consuming 40% less power in 15-watt mode at the same peak-to-peak performance.
- The module and developer kit will be available in the first half of 2027.
