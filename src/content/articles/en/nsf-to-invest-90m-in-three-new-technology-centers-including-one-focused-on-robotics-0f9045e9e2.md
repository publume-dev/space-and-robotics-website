- The U.S. National Science Foundation is investing $90 million over five years to support three new Science and Technology Centers, with $6 million annually for each center.
- One of the new centers, the Center for Human and Robot Co-Adaptation led by the University of Texas at Austin, will study how people and robots can securely and effectively adapt to one another as service and assistive robots become more common in homes, hospitals, workplaces, and public spaces.
