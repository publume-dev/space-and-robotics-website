- Gen. Michael Guetlein, the Pentagon official overseeing Golden Dome for America, warned that the initiative faces a fiscal year 2027 funding gap unless Congress finds a new way to finance the program.
- The Pentagon is seeking about $17.5 billion for Golden Dome in 2027, but only $400 million is in the base defense budget; most of the money sits in a separate $350 billion reconciliation request encountering resistance on Capitol Hill.
- Guetlein said that if lawmakers do not find a solution, there is no funding for Golden Dome in 2027, though he later expressed confidence an agreement will be reached.
- Golden Dome currently has $22.5 billion in initial funding with 95% committed, has its first operational site, and construction is underway at other sites.
- The program has completed two classified tests described as highly successful; the 2027 test campaign will be more complex and build toward the 2028 operational demonstration.
- President Trump signed a national security presidential directive roughly three weeks ago giving Golden Dome broader access to government data, and an interagency exercise is planned this fall to address legal and operational authorities.
