- Rocket Lab developed GHOST (Global Hypersonic & Orbital Spaceport Technology), a containerized launch system that packages ground-support hardware and range-control systems into standard shipping containers for Electron and HASTE rockets.
- The first GHOST installation will be at the Pacific Spaceport Complex-Alaska on Kodiak Island under Launch Complex 4, with operational debut expected for a suborbital mission in 2027.
- The Alaska expansion is tied to a $266 million U.S. Space Force contract for 12 suborbital launches, with options for six more.
- Rocket Lab CEO Peter Beck said GHOST was developed in response to customer demand for more mobile launch operations, particularly for HASTE hypersonic test missions.
