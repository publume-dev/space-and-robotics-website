- The U.S. Army has awarded Stratom Inc. an industry team a Project Sustainment contract to develop the Tactical Autonomous Logistics Utility System (TALUS), a modular autonomous distribution system for resupplying dispersed forces in contested environments.
- TALUS is an 18-month prototype effort aimed at integrating and maturing the capability for soldier operational evaluation, including a planned Transformation in Contact unit Combat Training Center rotation.
- The total anticipated project value is $15.9 million, according to Stratom president and CEO Mark Gordon.
- Stratom's partners for TALUS include ND Defense, Forterra, GS Engineering, and Waltonen Engineering, with Forterra contributing its AutoDrive autonomy stack.
- The article does not provide specific details about the technologies used in TALUS, as Stratom CEO said they are not able to share specifics at this stage.
