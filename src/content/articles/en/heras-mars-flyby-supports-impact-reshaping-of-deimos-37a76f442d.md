- A new study led by Sabina Raducan proposes that Deimos was reshaped by a single 320-meter asteroid impact at about 45 degrees, excavating a southern basin and covering the moon with up to 200 meters of regolith, consistent with Hera flyby images.
- Hera's March 2025 Mars flyby captured images of Deimos that revealed additional buried craters, helping constrain impact simulations.
- The survival of pre-impact craters suggests Deimos has a highly porous, fractured interior, implying it may be a rubble-pile body.
- JAXA's MMX mission to study both Martian moons is scheduled to launch this autumn.
