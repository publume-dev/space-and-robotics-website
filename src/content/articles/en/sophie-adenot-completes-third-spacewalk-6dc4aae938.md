- Sophie Adenot and Jessica Meir completed US EVA-99 on 1 September 2026, lasting 6 hours and 49 minutes.
- During EVA-99, Adenot replaced a failed retroreflector on the Harmony module, collected samples for the MicroOrganisms experiment, and installed power and thermal jumpers; Meir replaced a failed HD camera and began preparation of a future upgrade to the Alpha Magnetic Spectrometer.
- This was Adenot's third spacewalk, bringing her total spacewalking time to 19 hours and 42 minutes across three EVAs in 15 days.
