- Einride and DAF Trucks announced a partnership to integrate Einride's autonomous driving system with DAF's truck platform.
- The partners plan to define and test the integration of Einride Drive with DAF's platform, collaborating with TNO, with initial tests on a DAF truck planned for this year.
- The partners plan to integrate and commission Einride's autonomous driving software in 2027.
- The partnership aims to support the large-scale commercialization of SAE Level 4 autonomous electric freight transportation.
