- BlackSky announced a new multiyear international contract, reported to be in the seven-figure range, covering persistent space-based monitoring and on-demand satellite tasking.
- The contract follows a pilot program and includes low-latency access to Gen-2 and Gen-3 imagery plus AI-enabled analytics.
- Gen-3 satellites deliver 35-centimeter-class resolution and can automatically identify vehicles, aircraft, vessels, and other objects.
- BlackSky reported a 200% year-over-year increase in international revenue in the second quarter of 2026.
- The contract value is only described as "seven-figure," and the customer has not been identified.

- **Watch:** BlackSky's CEO says the company plans to have eight Gen-3 satellites in orbit by the end of 2026, and 12 to 15 satellites to achieve hourly revisits — forward-looking statements, not current achievements.
