- Blue Canyon Technologies, part of RTX's Raytheon, introduced FleXbus, a mission-ready kit of spacecraft components that includes the FleXcore guidance, navigation, and control subsystem.
- FleXbus includes an avionics computer and Command & Data Handling system, GNC control algorithms, sensors and actuators, an electrical power system controller and batteries, payload and peripheral systems interfaces, and flight software.
- FleXbus supports any spacecraft structure without configuration constraints, with optional solar array drive assemblies, solar arrays, hinges, and select radio and propulsion solutions available.

The product introduction may simplify the design and integration of small satellites and spacecraft platforms by offering a flexible, ready-to-use component kit, but no customer orders or market adoption have been evidenced.
