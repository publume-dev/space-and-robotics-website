- The Defense Innovation Unit (DIU) and Space Development Agency (SDA) awarded contracts of undisclosed value to D-Orbit USA, Firefly Aerospace, and Katalyst Space for a 'deorbit-as-a-service' project to study satellite disposal options.
- The selected companies will begin preliminary design and risk-reduction activities, after which SDA and DIU will assess technical maturity, mission approach, schedule, and affordability before deciding which solution proceeds to a full on-orbit demonstration.
- Firefly Aerospace proposed using its Elytra maneuvering spacecraft to rendezvous with client satellites for disposal, leveraging hardware and software derived from its Blue Ghost lunar program; Elytra has yet to fly its first mission.
- Katalyst Space's Link robotic servicing vehicle launched July 3 on NASA's Swift Boost mission and is intended to rendezvous with the Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory, capture it with robotic arms, and raise its orbit; Link encountered attitude-control problems in late July but received new flight software to restore attitude control.
