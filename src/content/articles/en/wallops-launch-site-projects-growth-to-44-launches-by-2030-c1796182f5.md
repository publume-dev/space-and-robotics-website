- NASA's OIG report in June projects Wallops launches will grow from 17 in 2025 to 44 in 2030, including suborbital launches.
- Firefly Aerospace plans to launch its Alpha and Eclipse vehicles from Wallops starting as soon as 2027.
- Wallops Flight Facility director David Pierce said the facility is using Lean Six Sigma exercises with Rocket Lab to increase range efficiency, cutting support costs by a factor of two.
- Wallops launch operations have been moved under the authority of Kennedy Space Center as part of a recent agency restructuring.
- Local officials cited housing and community amenities as a bottleneck for Wallops workforce growth.
