- Each month from January through March in the 2025-26 winter had the lowest Western snow cover in NASA's MODIS satellite record since 2001.
- Tacoma Power uses Upstream Tech's HydroForecast, which incorporates NASA satellite data on snow cover and vegetation, to manage reservoir operations on the Cowlitz River.
- On April 8, Washington state placed every watershed, including the Cowlitz, under a drought emergency.
- In 2026, NASA became a formal partner in producing the U.S. Drought Monitor, taking its first turn authoring the assessment during the week of Aug. 17.
