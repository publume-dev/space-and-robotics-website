- On 1 September 2026, at a signing ceremony in Copenhagen, ESA and ispace-Europe celebrated the implementation of MAGPIE, ESA's first lunar rover.
- Built to operate for about 10 Earth days, MAGPIE will explore the Moon's south pole to detect water and other volatiles and analyze subsurface geology, carrying a drill, volatile analyzer, ground-penetrating radar, and neutron detector.
- The rover is scheduled to launch in 2029 aboard ispace's Mission 4 lunar lander, via a collaboration between ESA and JAXA.
- ESA selected ispace-Europe as prime contractor to build, develop, and fly MAGPIE; the mission is ESA's first lunar small mission under the Moon Explore initiative.
