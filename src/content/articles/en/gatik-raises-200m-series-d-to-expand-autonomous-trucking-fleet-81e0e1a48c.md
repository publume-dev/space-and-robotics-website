- Gatik raised $200 million in Series D funding, led by Qatar Investment Authority and Koch Disruptive Technologies, with participation from Millennium Management, ARK Invest, Intact Private Capital, and others.
- Gatik has over $600 million in contracted revenue and has completed 85,000 fully driverless orders with 99% on-time delivery.
- Gatik plans to use the new funding to expand its fleet from dozens of driverless trucks to thousands across North America.
