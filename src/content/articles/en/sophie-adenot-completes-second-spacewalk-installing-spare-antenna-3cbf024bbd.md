- ESA astronaut Sophie Adenot and NASA astronaut Anil Menon completed US EVA-98, lasting 6 hours 30 minutes.
- During the EVA, they installed and activated a spare Space-to-Ground Antenna (SGANT) and secured the failed antenna.
- With this spacewalk, Adenot has now accumulated 12 hours and 53 minutes of spacewalking time over two EVAs.
