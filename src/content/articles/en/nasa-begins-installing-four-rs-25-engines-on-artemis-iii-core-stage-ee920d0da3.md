- On Aug. 24, technicians at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center began installing the four RS-25 engines in the core stage of the SLS rocket for Artemis III.
- The four engines assigned to Artemis III are E2054, E2057, E2048, and E2052, all previously used on space shuttle missions.
- Engine 2048 flew on STS-129, which launched astronaut Randy Bresnik, and on STS-95 in 1998, which returned John Glenn to orbit.
