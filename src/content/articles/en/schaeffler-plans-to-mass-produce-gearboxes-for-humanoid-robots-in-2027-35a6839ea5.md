- Schaeffler has concluded extensive validation testing of its formed strain wave gearboxes for humanoid robots and will be ready to start mass production in 2027.
- The forming process completes manufacturing steps in seconds instead of minutes, cutting costs by more than 25% and material consumption by more than 75%.
- Schaeffler plans to start production in Germany and later expand to other regions.
