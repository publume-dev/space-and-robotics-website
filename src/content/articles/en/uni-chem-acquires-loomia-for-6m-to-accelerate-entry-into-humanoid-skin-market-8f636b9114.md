- Uni-Chem Co. is acquiring Loomia Technologies Inc. for about $6 million U.S., securing 100% of Loomia's shares, with completion expected on Oct. 15 through a reverse triangular merger.
- Loomia's LEL (Loomia Electronic Layer) technology enables flexible bonding of electronic circuits to cloth and leather, overcoming disconnection during bending with non-printed, low-resistance mesh technology.
- Loomia and R&Y are co-developing a tactile sensor that detects both vertical pressure and shear force, with R&Y planning to deliver the first engineering sample in January 2027.
