- The new capital is designated for accelerating U.S. defense, aerospace, and industrial manufacturing capacity.
- The round brought Hadrian’s valuation to $7.87 billion; it was the company’s second funding round of 2026, following a January round that valued it at $1.6 billion.
- Hadrian opened new factories in Mesa, Arizona, and Muscle Shoals, Alabama, bringing its capacity to nearly 3 million square feet across four sites.
- The company plans to open additional factories and expand into munitions and autonomous systems over the next year.
