- Paragon Space Development Corp. laid off 70 people after Northrop Grumman terminated its contract to provide life support systems for the HALO module, following NASA's indefinite halt of the Gateway program.
- The layoffs affected personnel in Arizona, Colorado, and Texas, and were part of a 'companywide restructuring' announced on Aug. 11.
- Paragon will retain the intellectual property rights to the life support technologies it had been developing for HALO and expects compensation from Northrop for closeout costs.
- Northrop announced it will fly some HALO-developed technologies, including power and avionics systems, on three Lunar Infrastructure Demo missions starting later this decade.
- Paragon intends to apply its life support expertise to NASA's lunar base plans and other government and commercial projects.
