- ESA's FLEX satellite has been fuelled at Europe's Spaceport in French Guiana, ahead of its planned launch on 15 September at 03:21 CEST (14 September at 22:21 local time).
- The FLEX satellite is equipped with the Fluorescence Imaging Spectrometer, which will measure the faint fluorescence emitted by plants during photosynthesis to assess vegetation health and stress.
- FLEX will deliver the first global maps of vegetation fluorescence at a spatial resolution of 300 m × 300 m.
- The satellite was loaded with 30 kg of propellant to support manoeuvres after separation and throughout its planned minimum 3.5-year mission.
- FLEX will launch together with Copernicus Sentinel-3C on a Vega-C rocket.
