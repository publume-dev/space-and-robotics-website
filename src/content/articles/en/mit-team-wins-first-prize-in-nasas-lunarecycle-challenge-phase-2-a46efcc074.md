- MIT's CERBERUZ team won the first prize in NASA's LunaRecycle Challenge Phase 2, receiving a total of $775,000 for winning both the prototype development and digital twin tracks.
- The CERBERUZ technology grinds mixed trash into a fine powder, repurposing materials such as Zotek foam as reinforcement for injection-mold finished parts or 3D-printing filament.
- Nine other teams received prizes, including second place prototype Terasynth ($225,000), most innovative prototype RECLAIM from Penn State ($50,000), and digital twin runner-up Moon Made ($125,000).
- LunaRecycle is a $3 million, two-phase competition in partnership with The University of Alabama Lee J. Styslinger Jr. College of Engineering.
