- NASA's COFFIES team has developed a machine-learning model that can predict the emergence of active regions on the Sun up to 12 hours before they appear.
- The model analyzes data from NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory, using a sliding-window transformer architecture to detect patterns in acoustic waves and magnetic field changes.
- The model is not yet operational for real-time forecasting; the team plans to validate it across more known solar events.
