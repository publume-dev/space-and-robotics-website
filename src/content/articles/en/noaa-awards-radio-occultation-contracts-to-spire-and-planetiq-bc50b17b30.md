- NOAA awarded Spire Global a $3.7 million contract to provide 4,600 daily GNSS radio occultation profiles and 1,200 temporal resolution enhancements.
- NOAA awarded PlanetiQ a $2.7 million contract to deliver 4,200 GNSS radio occultation profiles and 500 total electron content tracks per day.
- Spire and PlanetiQ will deliver radio occultation data from Sept. 18 through Dec. 1, 2026.
- NOAA plans to establish an IDIQ contract vehicle later this year with a ceiling of $2.35 billion over 10 years for commercial space-based environmental monitoring data.
- PlanetiQ completed testing of its next-generation GNOMES satellites in preparation for launch later this year.
- NOAA shares commercial weather data with NASA, the U.S. Air Force, and the U.S. Navy for numerical weather prediction and space weather applications.
