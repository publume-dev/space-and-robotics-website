- Teradyne Robotics has filed a patent infringement lawsuit against JAKA's German subsidiary, alleging infringement of multiple Universal Robots patents covering hardware and software.
- The case was filed with the Unified Patent Court (UPC) Local Division in Copenhagen, and a ruling could affect 17 EU member states and certain non-UPC countries including the U.K. and Spain.
- This is Teradyne Robotics' second major case against a Chinese robotics company in Europe in 2026, following a copyright lawsuit against Elite Robots' German subsidiary.
- Teradyne Robotics Group President Jean-Pierre Hathout stated that the company will enforce its IP rights against illegal copying and infringement across hardware and software.
- Universal Robots CTO David Brandt said the primary objective is to stop JAKA from copying and infringing IP, not financial compensation, and that JAKA is not the only robot that looks suspiciously like theirs.
