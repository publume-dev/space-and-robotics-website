- The U.S. Space Force has added $28 million to an existing contract with Impulse Space to provide two more maneuverable spacecraft, Victus Salo 2 and Victus Salo 3, for tactically responsive space missions.
- The contract modification increases the total value to about $62.8 million over the original $34.8 million Phase 3 agreement, which covered Victus Surgo and Victus Salo 1.
- Victus Salo 2 and 3 will use Impulse's Mira spacecraft to host government-provided payloads in low Earth orbit, expanding the focus beyond rapid launch to include in-orbit positioning and rapid maneuvering.
- Victus Salo 1 is expected to launch in 2027 carrying a MIT Lincoln Laboratory payload, while Victus Surgo will test a Helios kick stage carrying Mira toward geosynchronous transfer orbit.
