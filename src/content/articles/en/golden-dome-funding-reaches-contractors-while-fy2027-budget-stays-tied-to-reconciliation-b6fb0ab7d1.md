- Northrop Grumman vice president Raymond Sharp said “money is flowing for Golden Dome” and that funding from budget reconciliation “opened up” during the second quarter.
- Parsons received a two-year, $514 million option under the Missile Defense Agency’s Technical, Engineering, Advisory and Management Support contract, covering advanced engineering for integrated missile defense including Golden Dome.
- Lockheed Martin CEO Jim Taiclet said a Lockheed directed-energy system had completed Golden Dome’s first milestone test, cueing, tracking, and engaging incoming threats.
- The administration’s roughly $17.5 billion Golden Dome request for fiscal year 2027 is almost entirely dependent on separate reconciliation legislation; regular defense appropriations supply about $400 million, leaving long-term funding uncertain.
- A 2025 reconciliation law supplied about $24 billion for integrated air and missile defense capabilities associated with Golden Dome.
- Golden Dome is a layered homeland defense architecture linking sensors, interceptors, and command systems across land, sea, air, and space.
- Most Golden Dome details remain classified, limiting public verification of contractor roles and system architecture.
