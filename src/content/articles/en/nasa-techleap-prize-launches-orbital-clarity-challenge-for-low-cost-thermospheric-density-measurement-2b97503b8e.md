- The Orbital Clarity Challenge is the sixth in the TechLeap Prize series, run by NASA's Heliophysics Division, Flight Opportunities program, and Center of Excellence for Collaborative Innovation, seeking low-cost methods to measure thermospheric density, pressure, or drag in low Earth orbit.
- The challenge plans to advance up to four winners' concepts to a flight-ready solution within 12 months, with each winning team offered a test flight at no cost.
- Up to four winners may receive up to $500,000 in prizes across three phases.
- Phase 1 registration closes on October 28, 2026, and submission closes on November 11, 2026.
