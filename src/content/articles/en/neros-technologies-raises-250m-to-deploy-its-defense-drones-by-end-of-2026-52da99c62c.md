- **Funding and valuation**: Neros Technologies raised $250 million in a Series C round, bringing its valuation to $2.5 billion.
- **Deployment plan**: The funding will accelerate development and production of the Archer AI FPV drone and Bandit c-UAS interceptor, with deployment in combat theaters planned by the end of 2026.
- **Existing contracts**: Neros has contracts with the U.S. Army, Marine Corps, every component of SOCOM, and half a dozen allied countries.
- **Production target**: Neros plans to produce 1 million drones per year by 2028, leveraging vertically integrated manufacturing and domestic production capacity.
