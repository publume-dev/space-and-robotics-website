- NASA's SpEED Demon sounding rocket launched from Wallops Flight Facility on Aug. 24, 2022, deploying four ejectable dropsondes plus the main payload to achieve the first concurrent multi-point sampling inside a sporadic E layer.
- Data from the mission revealed unexpected complexity in the sporadic E layer, showing uneven structure influenced by neutral winds and turbulence rather than a smooth dense layer, and the layer split into two peaks on descent consistent with Kelvin-Helmholtz billows.
- SpEED Demon was a technology demonstration that validated the dropsonde technique, enabling subsequent multi-probe missions during the October 2023 annular eclipse, April 2024 total solar eclipse, and the June 2025 SEED mission from Kwajalein Atoll.
