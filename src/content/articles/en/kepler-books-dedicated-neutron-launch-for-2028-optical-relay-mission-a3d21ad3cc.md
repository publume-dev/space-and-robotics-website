- Kepler's 2028 Neutron mission will be its first dedicated flight on the rocket, with the number of satellites undisclosed.
- The company deployed 10 Tranche 1 satellites of roughly 300 kilograms each earlier this year.
- Tranche 2 optical relay satellites are designed to support up to 100 Gbps data rates while remaining compatible with the earlier generation.
- Kepler is building 10 satellites for the first element of ESA's HydRON program as part of Tranche 2.
- Neutron is designed to carry up to 13,000 kilograms to low Earth orbit in its reusable configuration.
- Rocket Lab plans to debut Neutron late this year after a first-stage propellant tank ruptured during testing in January.
