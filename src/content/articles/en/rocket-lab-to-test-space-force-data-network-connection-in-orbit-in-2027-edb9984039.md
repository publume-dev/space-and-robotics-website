- Rocket Lab plans to use a Photon spacecraft for an in-orbit demonstration of the U.S. Space Force's Space Data Network in 2027.
- The contract is valued at $12 million and covers design and ground testing ahead of the flight; the Photon spacecraft will be equipped with optical communications and networking hardware to demonstrate interoperability.
- The Space Systems Command selected Rocket Lab as one of five companies for a $60 million effort to demonstrate how different commercial satellite systems can connect to the Space Data Network, with Amazon LEO for Government, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, and York Space Systems each receiving $12 million.
