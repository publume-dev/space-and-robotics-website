- On Aug. 7, the EU sanctioned five Russian defense industry executives, including Viktor Ivanov, head of Roscosmos subsidiary NII TP, which develops the Kasatka-R synthetic aperture radar system for the Russian military.
- The sanctions freeze any assets Ivanov holds in the EU, prohibit EU persons and entities from making funds available to him, and bar him from entering or transiting through EU territory.
- The EU also sanctioned Sergey Bashkov, director of Irz-Svyaz, a manufacturer of navigation receivers for GPS and GLONASS, and Aleksandr Dyukarev, director general of Krasnoyarsk Machine-Building Plant, which manufactures the RS-28 Sarmat intercontinental ballistic missile and modules for carrier rockets.
