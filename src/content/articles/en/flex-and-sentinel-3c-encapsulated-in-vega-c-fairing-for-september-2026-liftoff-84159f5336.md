- FLEX and Sentinel-3C have been enclosed within the Vega-C fairing at Europe's Spaceport.
- Liftoff is scheduled for 15 September 2026 at 03:21 CEST.
- Deployment order: Sentinel-3C will be released first from the upper part of the Vespa adapter, followed by FLEX.
- FLEX carries a Fluorescence Imaging Spectrometer to map vegetation fluorescence at 300×300-meter resolution.
- Copernicus Sentinel-3C will continue systematic monitoring of Earth's oceans, land, ice, and atmosphere, measuring parameters such as sea-surface temperature and ocean color.

The next major milestone is hoisting the encapsulated fairing atop the Vega-C rocket at the launch pad, signaling the final stage of pre-launch preparations.
