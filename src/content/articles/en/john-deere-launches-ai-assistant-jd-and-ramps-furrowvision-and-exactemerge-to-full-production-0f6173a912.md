- John Deere announced JD, a conversational AI tool in Operations Center that lets farmers ask open-ended questions about historical farm data and receive answers to improve yields and profitability.
- JD will be free to all Operations Center users, with early access opening in fall 2026 and broader access expanding over the next year.
- FurrowVision and ExactEmerge planter upgrades are entering full production for all new planter systems, following pilot deployments in the 2025/2026 growing season.

The free availability of JD may lower barriers to AI adoption for farmers, while full production of FurrowVision and ExactEmerge suggests broader planting accuracy improvements.
