- NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope launched aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy from Kennedy Space Center on Aug. 30, 2026.
- One hour and 23 minutes after launch, the solar panels and sunshade deployed successfully, powering the observatory and shading it from the Sun.
- Roman will survey billions of stars and galaxies with a field of view far larger than Hubble's, aiding studies of dark energy, exoplanets, and cosmic evolution.
- Roman is on a three-month journey to Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 2 (L2), where it will enter a stable halo orbit.
