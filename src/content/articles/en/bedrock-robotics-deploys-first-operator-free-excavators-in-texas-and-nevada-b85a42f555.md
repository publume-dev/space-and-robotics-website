- Bedrock Robotics has deployed fully autonomous, operator-free excavators on active infrastructure projects in Texas and Nevada.
- The first autonomous excavator projects include a water treatment facility with Sundt Construction in Nevada, a multi-million cubic yard earthwork site with Champion Site Prep in Texas, and a 1.2 million cubic yard civil sitework project with Zachry Construction Corp.
