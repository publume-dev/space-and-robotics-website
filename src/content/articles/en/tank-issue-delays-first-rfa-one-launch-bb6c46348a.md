- RFA encountered an issue with its RFA ONE vehicle during pad testing at SaxaVord Spaceport, requiring engineers to destack the vehicle.
- SaxaVord canceled the five-week launch window in August and September that had been reserved for the first RFA ONE flight.
- OHB CEO Marco Fuchs said engineers needed to examine the rocket's tanks after the issue and opened the tank in the hangar.
- Fuchs added that RFA still plans to conduct its first orbital launch attempt later this year.
- The exact nature and cause of the tank issue have not been disclosed, and the timeline for returning the rocket to the pad and the revised launch date are not specified.
