- The U.S. Space Force awarded $60 million to five companies to demonstrate how satellites from multiple vendors can connect to its Space Data Network.
- Amazon LEO for Government, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Rocket Lab, and York Space Systems each received $12 million in contracts and prototype agreements.
- Each company received a $10 million fixed-price contract for work lasting up to nine months to prototype and demonstrate connections among multiple vendors across the SDN backbone.
- The remaining $2 million to each company comes through six-month Other Transaction Authority agreements supporting the Space Force's planned 'space exchange point' concept.
- The work will test connections between commercial satellite systems and the Space Data Network, including on-orbit data transport and standardized interfaces.
