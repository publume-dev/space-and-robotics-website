- On Aug. 12, 2026, a total solar eclipse darkened skies over Greenland, Iceland, and Spain.
- NASA's WB-57F research jet flew at an altitude of 50,000 feet through the eclipse's shadow, carrying cameras that captured high-resolution images of the corona and prominences in multiple wavelengths.
- Teams of students participating in the NASA-funded Nationwide Eclipse Ballooning Project launched scientific balloons in Iceland and Spain to study the eclipse's effects on the atmosphere.

These observations are expected to yield data on the corona and atmospheric effects in the coming months.
