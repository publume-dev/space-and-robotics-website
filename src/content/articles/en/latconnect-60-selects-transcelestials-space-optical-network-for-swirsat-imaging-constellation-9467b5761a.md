- LatConnect 60 has signed a commercial contract with Transcelestial for an optical communications terminal for its SWIRSAT-1 mission and access to Transcelestial's optical ground station network.
- The terminal is designed for data rates up to 10 Gbps.
- Transcelestial currently operates two optical ground stations in Singapore and Spain, with five to six targeted by end of 2026.
- Two SWIRSATs are planned to launch in Q1 2027, with an 18-satellite constellation by 2029.
