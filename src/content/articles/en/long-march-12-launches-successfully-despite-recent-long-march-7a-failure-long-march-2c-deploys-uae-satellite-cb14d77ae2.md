- Long March 12 launched successfully on August 16, 2026, carrying the 24th operational batch of Guowang satellites, bringing the total in orbit to 195.
- The launch occurred less than a week after a Long March 7A failure; its use of YF-100K engines suggests the anomaly was not engine-related.
- A Long March 2C launched the UAE's 'Satellite for Earth Observation' (SEO) on August 17, 2026, from Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center.

The cause of the Long March 7A failure and its impact on future launch schedules remain unclear, but the Long March 12's success suggests the YF-100 engine family may not be affected.
