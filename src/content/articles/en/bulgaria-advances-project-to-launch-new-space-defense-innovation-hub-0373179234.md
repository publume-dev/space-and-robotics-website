- Bulgaria's government approved a draft memorandum of understanding with EnduroSat to develop a space and defense innovation hub at Dobroslavtsi Air Base.
- The government plans to build a National Center for Observation, Coordination and Management through Space Data.
- EnduroSat aims to create a comprehensive center for assembly, testing, and qualification of new generations of satellites at Dobroslavtsi.

The MoU is still a draft, and the final agreement, funding, and construction timeline have not been detailed, which may affect project progress.
