- NASA awarded Blue Origin a firm-fixed-price contract to develop the Mars Telecommunications Network, with a maximum value of approximately $700 million.
- Blue Origin will deliver a high-performance Mars telecommunications orbiter to NASA no later than Dec. 31, 2028.
- The network is expected to be operational at Mars by 2030.
