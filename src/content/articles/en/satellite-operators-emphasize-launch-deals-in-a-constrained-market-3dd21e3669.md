- AST SpaceMobile has booked 10 launches with two different providers, targeting a launch cadence of every month or two on average.
- The initial constellation deployment is delayed to early 2027; the launch plan depends on some New Glenn flights because Falcon 9 carries only three BlueBird satellites versus up to eight on New Glenn.
- HawkEye 360 has all launches locked in through 2028 and is considering other launch providers beyond that timeframe.
- Spire Global has launch capacity reserved through 2028 to maintain its collection capacity.
