- ESA's Cluster satellite Samba reentered Earth's atmosphere on 31 August 2026 at 21:39:38 UTC as the third of four satellites in a targeted reentry over the South Pacific Ocean.
- The airborne ROSIE observation team captured Samba's reentry with 29 of 30 instruments, and the final reentry prediction was accurate to the second.
- ESA began a targeted reentry campaign for Samba in November 2024 and adjusted the trajectory in January 2026 to enable airborne observation, applying lessons from the previous Salsa and Rumba reentries.
- ESA expects to release more imagery and video footage from the Samba reentry after the Tango reentry is observed on 1 September 2026 at 21:30:31 UTC.

Analysis of the collected data is expected to improve reentry models and design-for-demise engineering, though final scientific results are pending.
