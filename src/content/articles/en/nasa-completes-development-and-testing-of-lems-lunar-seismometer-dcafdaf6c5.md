- LEMS is the first payload NASA designed for Artemis astronauts to deploy on the lunar surface; its hardware development and environmental testing are complete.
- It is about the size of a small suitcase, weighs 11 pounds under lunar gravity, and carries two compact, sensitive seismometers to monitor moonquakes and meteorite impacts.
- The payload passed environmental tests verifying it can survive launch vibration, the lunar temperature and radiation environment, and is safe for astronaut handling.
- LEMS is designed to operate through the two-week lunar night without external power or heat sources, using advanced insulation, low-thermal-conductivity cables, and a thermal regulator to withstand temperatures as low as minus 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
- LEMS will remain at NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center until it is assigned to an Artemis mission for deployment near the lunar South Pole.

- Watch for: NASA assignment of LEMS to a specific Artemis mission; no mission or launch date has been announced.
