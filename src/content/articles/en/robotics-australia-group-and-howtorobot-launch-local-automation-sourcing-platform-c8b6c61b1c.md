- Robotics Australia Group and HowToRobot launched AutomationFinder.com.au, a localized version of HowToRobot's AI-powered automation sourcing platform for Australia.
- Australia's robot density was 134 robots per 10,000 manufacturing employees, below the world average of 162, per the International Federation of Robotics.
- AutomationFinder.com.au includes a network of over 20,000 vetted suppliers, with more than 500 based in Australia.
- The platform draws on data from over 600 existing automation projects and offers access to independent automation experts for engagements in Australia.
