- Space Angel has been awarded the $1.75 million Spaceport Establishment Support Grant by the Western Australian Government.
- The funding supports site assessments and the development of a business case for the proposed Western Australian spaceport.
- The grant is part of the Western Australian Government's $2 million Made in WA commitment to accelerate local space industry capability.
