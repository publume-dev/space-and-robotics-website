- NASA's Deep Space Station 23 (DSS-23), a new 34-meter beam-waveguide antenna, began operations at Goldstone on August 3, 2026, following a testing campaign from May to July.
- The antenna has since communicated with missions including Chandra X-ray Observatory, Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, Psyche, Juno, and Voyager 1.
- The Deep Space Network's Aperture Enhancement Project will complete with Deep Space Station 33 at Canberra in 2029, bringing the total number of 34-meter antennas across the network to 13.
