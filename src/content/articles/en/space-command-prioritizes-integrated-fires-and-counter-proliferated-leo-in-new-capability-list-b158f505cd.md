- U.S. Space Command's Integrated Priorities List for fiscal years 2029-2033 ranks integrated space fires and countering proliferated LEO constellations as the top two capability needs.
- General Stephen Whiting announced the priorities on Aug. 12 at the Space and Missile Defense Symposium, describing the list as a demand signal to military services, labs, and industry.
- The list also includes joint integrated space command and control, dedicated space domain awareness, and sustained space maneuver as the third, fourth, and fifth priorities.
- Whiting stated that existing U.S. policy against testing debris-producing direct-ascent anti-satellite weapons remains in force, and Space Command will operate within those restrictions.
- Whiting emphasized that countering space-enabled targeting is not exclusively a Space Force mission, and Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps units may need their own capabilities.
