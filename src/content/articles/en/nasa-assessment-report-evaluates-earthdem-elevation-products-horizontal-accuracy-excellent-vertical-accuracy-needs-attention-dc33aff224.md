- A new quality assessment report from NASA's Commercial Satellite Data Acquisition (CSDA) program evaluates Polar Geospatial Center (PGC) EarthDEM elevation products.
- Horizontal accuracy: 0.5 m RMSEH for Strip DSM and 0.3 m RMSEH for Mosaic Tile, both graded 'Excellent.'
- Vertical accuracy: 5.6 m RMSEV for Strip DSM and 4.9 m RMSEV for Mosaic Tile, both exceeding PGC specification of 0.5 m RMSEV, leading to a 'Basic' compliance grade.
- Vertical accuracy varies by land cover type and cloud cover.
- The assessment supports use of EarthDEM data for NASA Earth science research as long as data characteristics such as vertical accuracy, cloud masking, and data voids are compatible with specific science objectives.
