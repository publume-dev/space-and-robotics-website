- MTG-I2 launched on Ariane 6 flight VA270 on 27 August 2026 at 22:11 CEST from Europe's Spaceport in French Guiana.
- It carries the Flexible Combined Imager and Lightning Imager, enabling rapid-scan imagery every 2.5 minutes over Europe and northern Africa.
- The launch was the ninth Ariane 6 flight and the first to place a satellite into geostationary transfer orbit.
- After commissioning, MTG-I2 will complete the three-satellite MTG constellation, enabling full-capacity operation.
