- Carbon Robotics replaced its crop-specific AI models with a single large plant model trained on 150 million labeled plants, enabling farmers to customize the LaserWeeder in minutes via an iPad app.
- The company partnered with iMerit to annotate over one million plant images for training the model, after initially labeling only 2,000 images internally.
- Carbon Robotics released Carbon ATK, an autonomy kit that retrofits John Deere 6R, 8R, 8RX, 8RT (2019+) tractors without permanent modifications, enabling autonomous weeding, tillage, spraying, and harvesting.
